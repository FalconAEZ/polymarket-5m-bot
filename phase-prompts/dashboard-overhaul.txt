THE WORK — three phases

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE A — Critique (15-30 min)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Audit the current dashboard against:

1. **Information hierarchy.** Are the most-important numbers (PnL,
   fire rate, mode banner) actually the most prominent? Are they
   visually weighted vs secondary detail (per-decision rows, log lines)?

2. **At-a-glance state communication.** Open the URL, time yourself:
   how long does it take to know
     a) is the bot firing?
     b) are we making money?
     c) is anything broken?
   If any answer takes >2 seconds the design failed that question.

3. **Density vs whitespace.** Streamlit's default tries to fill every
   pixel. Where do we currently feel cramped? Where could whitespace
   create breathing room?

4. **Color usage.** Is red/yellow/green used consistently for
   bad/warn/good? Are gradients adding value or noise? Is the dark
   theme legible (contrast ratios)?

5. **Animation.** Right now: zero. Where would tasteful motion help
   (e.g., counter rolls when PnL updates, gentle fade on auto-refresh,
   trip-wire bar fills approaching breach)?

6. **Empty states.** When the bot first starts (no decisions yet),
   does the dashboard look broken, or does it explain itself?

7. **Mobile / narrow viewport.** Does it degrade gracefully? Operator
   may want to glance at it from phone.

8. **Future-scaling.** What breaks when we add a second market? A
   second strategy? More than 4 services?

Capture findings in `runbooks/research/dashboard-critique-<date>.md`.
