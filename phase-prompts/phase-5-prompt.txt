-------------------------------------------
This is the gate test for whether Dublin was the right region.

    ping -c 50 ws-subscriptions-clob.polymarket.com

    time curl -i -N --max-time 5 \
        -H "Connection: Upgrade" \

        https://ws-subscriptions-clob.polymarket.com/ws/market

Pass criteria per RUNBOOK 5.3:
