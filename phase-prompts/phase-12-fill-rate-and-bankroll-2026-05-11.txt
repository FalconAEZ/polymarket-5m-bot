ITEM 2 — Lift fill rate to ~100% via strategy adjustment (~1-2h)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Once Item 1 categorizes the unfills, fix the fixable ones. Two main
levers:

(a) **Lift price_limit by N cents** — currently the bot fires at
    spot-derived price (e.g. limit=0.55 vs ask=0.56 means we miss).
    Bumping limit by +1¢ buys most of the misses. Trade-off: paying
    1¢ more per fill on every fill (not just the misses).

(b) **Take-at-ask mode** — set price_limit = best_ask + impact at
    decision time. Always crosses the spread, always fills.
    Trade-off: higher execution cost vs maker fills.

Recommended: option (a) with a tunable `SPOT_AWARE_LIMIT_LIFT_BPS`
env var. Default 0; operator can dial up to 1-3¢ to see the impact.

For the LLM-FIRE path specifically, consider option (b) — when the LLM
says FIRE the bot is more confident, so paying the spread is worth it.

Tests:
  * Lift = 0¢ → no behavior change
  * Lift = 2¢ → fill rate climbs, WR holds within 1-2pp
  * Per-strategy override (LLM-FIRE gets bigger lift than wide-fire base)

Verification on the bot:
  * After deploy, fill rate should climb from 74% → 90%+
  * WR may dip 1-2pp (we're now taking marginal trades that were
    correctly being skipped)
  * Net PnL/trade should be similar or higher

Don't go to 100% if it crashes WR. Find the sweet spot.

(b) **Position sizing**: change $5 flat to a fraction of bankroll
    (e.g. 0.5%, 1%, 2%). Implementation: add `POSITION_SIZING_MODE`

(c) **Kelly sizing**: per locked decision #9, fractional Kelly when
    edge is high. Need: (1) edge estimate per fire (already have
    p_win from spot_aware_v2); (2) Kelly formula `f = (p*b - q)/b`
    where b=odds, p=win prob, q=1-p; (3) cap at half-Kelly for
    variance control.

    Implementation: `kelly_size_usd(p_win, fill_price, bankroll)` in
    a new strategies/sizing.py module. Tests for known cases.

Anti-vibe: don't ship all three at once. Start with (a) — read-only
display change, no behavior risk. Then (b) flat-fractional. Then
Kelly with explicit operator approval (this is a locked-decision
