INITIAL LAUNCH RAN INTO A REAL BUG (slow-feed starvation):
  After 3 minutes of capture, oracle_*.parquet was missing while
  health.feeds.oracle.msgs_seen showed 5 records and queue_depth was 0.
  The records had been pulled from the queue but never flushed to disk.

  Root cause: the writer's batch-size flush (per-key, when a single
  source's pending list reaches 200 records) was incorrectly resetting
  last_flush. Polymarket at ~285 records/s hit batch_size every ~0.7s,
  meaning the time-based _flush_all sweep that was supposed to fire
  every 5s NEVER fired. Slow feeds (oracle at ~33s/event) accumulated
  records but never reached batch_size of 200 in any reasonable time
  and never got flushed.

  Surfacing this to the operator was load-bearing: a 7-day capture
  with no oracle file means the entire purpose of Phase 2 (validating
  the oracle heartbeat thesis) is silently broken.

  Fix committed in 2454c14 with a regression test that reproduces the
  original failure (600 polymarket + 1 oracle records, assert both
  files exist).
