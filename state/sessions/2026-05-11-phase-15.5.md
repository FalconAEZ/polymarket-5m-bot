## Answer to Item 1e — bot's accounting OR audit scripts?

**Audit scripts.** The bot's decision-log writer is correct:
- It writes `settled` at observation time using whatever resolution the local oracle reports.
- It writes `settled_correction` when post-hoc reconciliation (Polymarket API or phantom-fill backfill) overrides the earlier truth.

The bug class is on the *read* side: any audit that does `sum(r["pnl_usd"] for r in jsonl if r["kind"]=="settled")` gets the pre-correction number. The v4 dashboard correctly honors corrections (which is why it reports $75.80, close to the truth). The Phase 13 / Phase 14 / deep-dive audits did NOT honor corrections — which is the entire source of the operator's "$2,504 phantom" framing.

**Recommended fix shape (NOT shipped in Item 1):**
- Add `decisions.iter_effective_settled() -> Iterator[SettledEffective]` to the decision-log read API. It should fold `settled_correction` rows into the matching `settled` rows last-write-wins, returning a single effective record per `decision_id`.
- All audits + the dashboard's PnL accessor route through it.
- Phase 16's bankroll-display scenarios panel (Item 3) MUST use this accessor.

That's a small, surgical refactor — likely 50-100 lines + a regression test. Not in scope for Item 1; flag in the operator-go discussion.

- **No new phantom mechanism on May 10.** All 45 corrections cluster on May 10, but they are *retroactive* on May 9 windows (the backfill ran on May 10 against the May 9 incident). May 10 itself produced 181 settled rows with normal-looking PnL.
- **No reconciliation_mismatch.** Every settled row's `pnl_usd` matches the recomputed `(size × payoff − fee)`. The writer's arithmetic is sound.
