### Bonereaper microstructure (commit pending)

After the operator sent screenshots from predicts.guru showing Bonereaper's $12.8M net profit, 52.6% win rate, and a price-tier breakdown where >70% entries print 95% win rate and +7.3% ROI, formed two sequential hypotheses (BOTH WRONG):

1. **Hypothesis 1: Market making.** Killed by the predicts.guru `BUY/SELL RATIO: 5000 / 0`. Bonereaper never closes positions early; market makers post both sides and capture spread by closing.
2. **Hypothesis 2: Late-window favorite sniping.** Their >70¢ entries should cluster in the final 30-60s exploiting spot-vs-book lag. Killed by the microstructure data: only **15.5%** of >=70% entries land in the final 60s.

Pulled 60,500 of bonereaper's BTC 5-min trades over 7 days via [[../../scripts/research/bonereaper_microstructure]]; full report at [[../../runbooks/research/bonereaper-microstructure-2026-05-07]]. Headline findings:

- Bonereaper enters **EARLY**, not late — biggest bucket (27.6%) is the first minute of the window.
- Coverage is real: 1,950 of 2,017 markets in 7 days = **96.7%** participation. Operator's instinct that "they enter every 5-min window" is correct.
- Per-trade edge is **tiny everywhere** — 0.5-1.9% gross PnL across price tiers. The money comes from **volume × selective sizing on favorites** ($1.5M of $2.76M notional concentrated in the 85-100% tier).
- Overall 7-day picture: $37,558 PnL on $2.76M notional = **1.36% gross ROI per trade-week**. The "high win rate" framing was wrong; their net 52.3% win rate is barely above coin-flip.
- We **don't yet know what the actual signal is**. The 1-3% edge per trade in the 70-100% tier is real but small; the source (better spot data, order-flow reading, fee tier rebates, something else) is still a mystery.

### What this means for our project

Both my hypotheses (market maker, late-window sniper) were wrong. Operator's "every 5-min window" instinct was directionally right on COVERAGE but wrong on the implied "high win rate." Bonereaper's strategy is volume-traded probabilistic edge harvesting, not high-confidence directional prediction.

**Do NOT pivot the bot.** We don't have:
- The capital scale ($329 avg × 4,569 favorite-tier trades/week ≈ $1.5M notional/week — our $1k bankroll gets nowhere)
- The actual signal (whatever it is — spot mispricing? order flow? rebates?)
- The fee tier (Polymarket maker rebates almost certainly negotiated at $80M lifetime volume)

**Immediate honest conclusion:** Phase 8 ensemble revisit and decision #19 un-archiving both still need fresh design work backed by spot-vs-book mispricing analysis. Not pursuing as next code task. Bot continues paper soak.

### Coda 3 — spot-vs-book correlation analysis (commit pending)

Operator green-lit running the spot-vs-book correlation analysis after confirming it wouldn't affect the soak. Built [[../../scripts/research/bonereaper_spot_vs_book]] which joins the 60,500 bonereaper trades against captured Binance ticks (from `bold-galileo-2d56ca/ticks/`, ~132 min of overlap).

**Hypothesis: Bonereaper uses BTC spot-vs-window-open as a side-selection signal.** **SUPPORTED by data**, with caveats. Full report: [[../../runbooks/research/bonereaper-spot-vs-book-2026-05-07]].

Headline numbers (837 of 60,500 trades joined):
- **Aligned** (UP+Δ>0 OR DOWN+Δ<0): 459 trades, **78.0%** win rate, +$7.10/trade.
- **Contrarian** (opposite): 334 trades, **25.1%** win rate, -$4.52/trade.
- Win-rate gap: **+52.8 percentage points**.

Cleaner per-bucket — when BTC has moved 5-10bps in the first part of a window:
- UP at +5..+10bps: 29 trades, 93.1% win rate.
- UP at -10..-5bps: 15 trades, 0% win rate.
- DOWN at -10..-5bps: 15 trades, 100% win rate.
- DOWN at +5..+10bps: 15 trades, 0% win rate.

Interpretation: the signal isn't magic. It's "**Polymarket book lags BTC spot**" + "**5-min BTC has momentum**" — both small effects individually, multiplicative when combined. Aligned-trade gross ROI on notional is ~6.5%; after Polymarket fees + slippage, real edge is probably 3-5%. Still tradeable.
