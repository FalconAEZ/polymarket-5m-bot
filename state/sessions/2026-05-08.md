### #2 — "We should be entering every 5-min window aggressively for high win-rate"

**No, that's an internally-inconsistent statement that would lose money systematically.** Three corrections:

1. **The threshold IS the strategy.** Decision #6 locked `composite ≥ 0.6` because the bot only has edge when window-delta + oracle-gap signals agree strongly. Setting threshold = 0 = trading on coin-flip noise. With Polymarket fees + slippage, a 50% win-rate strategy is a guaranteed losing strategy. Break-even is around 52-54%; "interesting" is ≥57%.
2. **Volume and win-rate are independent.** Bonereaper enters aggressively *because they have edge*; edge × volume = wins amplified. No edge × volume = losses amplified faster. We don't yet have empirical evidence of edge.
3. **Zero decisions in 30 min = 5-window sample.** Statistically meaningless. Real soak is days. If after 24-48h still zero, the answer is **a replay calibration**, not "lower the threshold to 0."

Locked decisions in play: #6, #16, #19. Lowering the threshold means revisiting decision #6 with a written justification + backtest evidence per anti-vibe rule #1. **Period.**
